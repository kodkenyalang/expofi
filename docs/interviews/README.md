## Privacy first: this repo is public

- **Never commit names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, or society names** without written permission. Use a code (`C1`, `C2`, …) and a type ("allotment association, ~120 plots").
- Raw notes and recordings stay in a private place (not this repo). Only the anonymised summary goes in `docs/interviews/Cn.md`.
- Ask before recording. If they say no, take notes.
- Tell them what it's for: a hackathon project that might become a product. No selling.

## Who to talk to

Coordinators or volunteers who **already run** a seed library, seed swap, or allotment seed tin. They have the job today. People who only like the idea don't count toward M10.

Places they are found: allotment association committees, community seed libraries (often in public libraries), seed swap events, community garden groups, and local gardening clubs. Reach out as a person asking for 20 minutes of their experience, not as a pitch.

## The script (20–30 minutes)

Ask about **what happened**, not what they would do. Past behaviour is evidence; opinions about a future app are not. Don't show the product until the last five minutes, if at all.

**Warm-up**

1. How does your library or swap work today, from someone taking seed to seed coming back?
2. What do you keep track of, and where (paper, spreadsheet, group chat)?

**The returns problem** (does the problem exist?)

3. Think of last season. Roughly how much of what went out came back as saved seed?
4. Tell me about the last time seed didn't come back. What happened next?
5. What did you do about it? Did anything change for that person?

**Decision 1: who can borrow** (`join` is open today)

6. How does someone become a member? Has anyone ever been turned away or asked to wait?

**Decision 2: who confirms a return** (borrower alone, or steward too)

7. When seed comes back, who checks it? What do they look at?
8. Has anyone returned seed that turned out to be the wrong thing, or not viable?

**Decision 3: crop failure** (`forgive` is not built)

9. What happens when someone's crop fails and they have nothing to return?
10. Should that person be able to borrow again next season? Who decides?

**Decision 4: who pays** (a member currently pays ~0.0063 SOL of account rent per borrow-and-return)

11. Does the library or society have any budget, or a membership fee? What is it spent on?
12. Would members expect to pay anything to borrow? (Note the reaction, not just the answer.)

**Lineage** (is the "offspring" record valuable?)

13. Do you know where your oldest varieties came from? Does anyone ask?
14. If you could see that a bean descends from one grown here five seasons ago, would that change anything for you?

**Close**

15. Who else should I talk to?
16. Can I come back to you when there's something to try?
